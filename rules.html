Rounds

A round is one fairy's reviews on one PR, grouped by the commit they looked at. Replies on the same commit never make a new round. A review on a new commit starts a new round, unless your current round on that PR began less than 12 hours earlier, so one sitting is one round.

A round is qualifying when it approves, requests changes, or starts at least one thread. Reviews on your own PRs do not count.

A gremlin is a thread you started that is now resolved and outdated, with an author push after the author could see it (once your review was submitted). It is the closest thing the data has to "this comment led to a change".

How fairy dust is made

size = min(4, 1 + log2(1 + lines / 200))

work = 10 x size + min(6, 2 x threads) + min(20, 4 x gremlins)

work = work x repeat factor (100%, 50%, 25%)

dust = work + swift + second sight + key turner + wish

  • Every round starts from 10 dust times the PR's size. Size grows slowly with the counted lines and stops at 4.
  • An approval with no threads and an empty comment is a light touch, so its size stops at 1.5.
  • Each thread you start adds 2, up to 6. Each gremlin adds 4, up to 20.
  • Coming back to the same PR still counts, but less: your rounds on one PR earn 100%, 50%, 25% of their work, in order, and later rounds keep the last factor.
  • Rounds that start after the PR was merged earn 10 dust and nothing more.

Bonuses

Swift +5
Your round ended a wait within 24 weekday hours.
Second sight +15
A second review. Your first qualifying round on a PR of at least 100 counted lines, qualifying before the merge, after another core fairy (not the author, not a bot or the Changeling) had already given it a qualifying review. Once per fairy per PR.
Key turner +5
Your round holds the approval that opened the gate. Each reviewer's latest approval or change request is replayed up to the merge, and a dismissal removes it. Of the approvals still standing at the merge from people who could merge (anyone but the author with push access, or a core fairy in records that do not say), the key goes to the most recent one.
Wish up to +30
Your round ended a wait. Every weekday day past the first 2 adds 2, up to 30.

Wishes, sleepers and the lost

A PR is waiting while it is open, ready, and the ball is in the reviewers' court. The wait starts when the PR opens ready, leaves draft or reopens. After a qualifying round, the next wait starts at the author's next push or review request. Re-requesting review mid-wait does not reset the clock.

The wait ends at the first qualifying round by anyone but the author. That fairy claims the wish. Drive-by comments do not end the wait and do not claim it.

A PR that has waited 14 days or more is a Sleeping Beauty. A single review breaks the spell.

A waiting PR with no author activity for 30 days is lost in the woods. Author activity is opening, leaving draft, reopening, pushing, requesting review or commenting on the PR. It stays in the well, apart from the rest, and grants no wish until the author is active again. A PR lost in the woods that never had a qualifying review still counts as waiting, and as a Sleeping Beauty, for the queue weather and the Circle quest's sleeper goal, so leaving a PR alone until it gets lost cannot improve either.

Called on a waiting PR lists the fairies with a pending review request on GitHub, and any team that was asked (named when GitHub shows us which team). A team request does not put the PR in anyone's summons.

The well whispers a suggested fairy for each waiting PR that is not lost in the woods, from the core fairies who are not the author and are not already called. A fairy who already had a round on the PR comes first, the most recent one first, since they know it best. Otherwise PRs with the brightest wish choose first, and each goes to the fairy with the lightest load who reviewed one of its crates in the last 180 days. Load is rounds in the last 7 days, plus pending GitHub review requests on open PRs, plus whispers already given, so the work spreads out. Ties go to whoever did the most rounds on PRs touching those crates in the last 90 days. Crate knowledge counts only each reviewed PR's primary crates. A whisper is a hint, not a review request, and earns no dust of its own.

Time is counted in weekday hours: Saturdays and Sundays do not count. No timezones are configured, so weekdays are counted in UTC for everyone.

Levels

Your level comes from the dust you made over the last 90 days, so it can rise and fall with the seasons.

  1. Sprout 0+ dust You have taken root. Welcome to the glade.
  2. Pixie 100+ dust Small wings, sharp eyes.
  3. Sprite 400+ dust The diffs have started to sparkle.
  4. Fairy 1,200+ dust Authors leave the porch light on for you.
  5. Elder Fae 3,000+ dust The old magic answers when you call.

The Circle quest

Once a moon the whole court tries to keep the circle whole. All of these must hold by the end of the moon:

  1. 85% of waits answered by a review within 2 weekday daysGoal: 85%
  2. 95% of merges into main by people had a human review firstGoal: 95%
  3. No more Sleeping Beauties than at the start of the moon, counting the PRs lost in the woods that never had a reviewGoal: at most 65

When a moon keeps every goal, every core fairy earns a Circle. While a moon is running, a share goal shows how far it is from flipping: how many on-time answers or reviewed merges in a row would win it back, or how many misses it can still take.

A merge into main counts as reviewed when someone other than its author left a review of any kind or a thread before the merge. Bots and the Changeling do not count as reviewers, and PRs opened by bots are left out. A merge without one is merged without a fairy, and the Garden, the profiles and the Weekly Glade Post name them.

Courts and crowns

A moon is a calendar month. A court is a season: Winter (December to February), Spring, Summer and Autumn. A round belongs to the period it started in. Periods before the first official court are marked unofficial (pre-season).

  • Each moon crowns the Fairy of the Moon (most dust) and the Brightest Glow: this moon's dust divided by the larger of 50 and your usual (the median of your previous 3 moons), for fairies with at least 50 dust. It rewards doing more than usual.
  • Each court crowns the Monarch (dust), the Wakener (wish dust), Swift Wings (median response, at least 5 answers, not counting answers that woke a Sleeping Beauty, which Curse Breaker rewards), the Wayfinder (distinct primary crates) and the Gremlin Crown (gremlins).
  • Nobody holds more than 2 crowns of the same court. The next in line takes the rest.
  • Each moon, a lonely grove is a crate with at most one core reviewer over the previous 90 days. The first other fairy to finish 3 rounds in it becomes its Grove Guardian.

The Changeling is the Cursor review bot. It is scored with the same rules and appears on the boards, but it never ranks and never wins. The Changeling reviews alongside the court and wins nothing. Be better than the Changeling.

Counted lines

A PR's size is its additions plus deletions, without lock files and generated files. Any single file with more than 2,000 changed lines is treated as generated.

A PR's primary crates are the crates that hold at least 20% of its counted lines, plus its largest crate. Pathfinder, the Wayfinder and the well's whispers count only primary crates, so one wide PR cannot sweep them.

Files that never count: Cargo.lock *.lock **/Cargo.lock **/*.lock
